Copyright & DMCA Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. Copyright contact for YTDown: agentmobee@gmail.com.

How YTDown treats copyright

YTDown does not host, store or index videos. It is a technical tool that, at a visitor's request, fetches a publicly available video from its original platform and converts it into a downloadable file for that visitor. Files created during a download are temporary and are deleted after delivery. We respect the rights of creators and expect visitors to only download material they own or are allowed to save, as set out in our Terms of Use.

If you are a rights holder

If you believe material accessible through this tool infringes your copyright, please tell us and we will act on valid requests, including blocking the specific link on this service where appropriate. Because the original file lives on the source platform (for example YouTube), removing it there removes it everywhere - you may also wish to file your notice with that platform.

What to include in a takedown notice

  • Your full name (or your organisation's) and contact details.
  • Which work you own the rights to, and proof or a statement of your authority to act.
  • The exact link (URL) involved.
  • A statement that you believe, in good faith, that the use is not authorised by you, your agent or the law.
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and your physical or electronic signature.

Send notices to agentmobee@gmail.com with the subject line "Copyright notice".

Repeat misuse

Links reported in valid notices may be blocked on this service, and we may refuse service to visitors who repeatedly misuse the tool.

What this site does

YTDown is a small tool that turns a public YouTube link you paste in into a file download. To do that, our server has to fetch the link you give us and the video data needed to prepare your file.

What we collect

  • The links you submit. A pasted link is used only to prepare your download. We do not build a profile of what you download, and we aim to delete the temporary working files created for a download shortly after it is delivered.
  • Basic technical logs. Like almost every web server, ours keeps short-lived logs (time of visit, pages requested, browser type, and IP address) so we can keep the service running and stop abuse. These are not sold.
  • No accounts. YTDown has no sign-up and asks for no name, password or payment details.

Cookies and advertising

The tool itself does not need tracking cookies to work. If advertising (for example Google AdSense) is added to this site in the future, the advertising provider may place its own cookies to show and measure ads, under that provider's privacy policy. Where the law requires it, a consent notice will be shown.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may pass through the services needed to run the site (hosting provider, and YouTube/Google when a link is fetched). We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your choices

You can use the site without giving us any personal details beyond a link. If you contact us at [your email], we will only use your message to reply. You may ask us what information, if any, we hold about you and ask for it to be deleted.

Changes

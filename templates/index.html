What can and cannot be downloaded

Normal public videos, Shorts and music videos work. Private videos, members-only or paid content, protected (DRM) films, playlists and channel pages, and live streams that are still running cannot be processed - open a single public video instead. The qualities offered depend on the original upload: if a video was only uploaded in 720p, nothing higher can be created from it.

Please respect creators. Only download videos that you made yourself, that you have the owner's permission to save, or that you are otherwise legally allowed to keep (for example Creative Commons or public-domain material). YTDown is a tool for personal, lawful use - what you save with it is your responsibility.

Quick questions

Is it really free? Yes - single video downloads are free, with no sign-up.

Why does an HD download take a moment? For 1080p and above, YouTube keeps picture and sound as separate tracks, so the server first joins them into one MP4 for you.

The file opened in the player instead of saving? On some phones, long-press the playing video or use the browser menu to save it, then try again from this page.

Why is my quality missing? Only the qualities present in the original upload can be offered.
